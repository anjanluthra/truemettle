FAQs

Do you invest in startups?
Not usually. We back businesses that have been trading for two years or more and are already profitable, with good fundamentals. If you’re earlier than that, send it anyway — everyone gets honest feedback.
How much do you invest?
Up to $50k. The cheque is the easy part. What comes with it, the playbooks and a team of founders and operators in your corner, is what grows the profit. We’ll go through the terms on the intro call.
Do I keep control?
Yes. We take a minority stake and work beside you. It’s your business; our job is to help you get more out of it.
What do I get beyond the money?
Operators in your corner: operations, AI, social media growth, hiring, running lean and profitably, and your finances and governance — plus Anjan’s media and a network of founders, investors and partners.
Is True Mettle a Fund?
No. True Mettle is the personal investment vehicle of Anjan Luthra, a seven-figure entrepreneur. It’s his own capital — no fund, no committee — so we move fast.

Cookie policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit this site and which pages they read. That’s the only third party on the site: no advertising, no ad tracking, and our fonts and images are served from here.

The cookies

Google Analytics sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_P4R91FQN2T, which tell visits apart and last up to two years. They hold a random ID, not your name or email, and we have advertising features switched off.

Visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google Analytics runs without cookies: it counts the visit, but sets nothing on your device and can’t recognise you next time.

Turning it off

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way.
