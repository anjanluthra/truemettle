I

What it is

True Mettle invests my own capital in businesses that already work — and I work alongside the owners to help them get more out of what they’ve built.

Mettle is what’s revealed under pressure, not before it. I back people who’ve been tested and kept going, because I’ve been tested myself.

II

What I invest in

Five things I look for:

  1. 01

    Cash flow positive — profitable or breakeven today, on real numbers.

  2. 02

    Proven demand — a full diary, a waiting list, a referral pipeline. Demand I didn’t have to create.

  3. 03

    Recurring or repeat revenue.

  4. 04

    Clear, unrealised upside — I can name where the value is before I’ve seen the accounts.

  5. 05

    An owner who wants a partner, not just a cheque.
